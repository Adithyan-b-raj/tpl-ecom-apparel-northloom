Radical transparency

What you actually pay for.

Most brands keep the maths private. We do not. Pick any piece and see where every rupee goes: the fabric, the people who cut and stitch it, freight, duties, GST and what is left for us.

Our margin is the smallest honest number we can run the business on. We publish it so you can decide if it is fair.
